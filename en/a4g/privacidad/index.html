Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

a4g (app4goals) is an app for reaching goals: a new habit, a project, whatever you set out to do. Each goal is a photo cut into pieces, and you place one for every day you move forward. It is made by apps4all, that is, APPS 4 ALL, S. L., tax ID B05682919, based at Josefina Mascareñas, 3, 08173 Sant Cugat del Vallès (Barcelona), Spain. For any question about your data, write to jcgualde@apps4all.app.

The short version

The app has no user account, no sign-up, and sends nothing to any server of ours. Everything you do in it is stored on your phone, and only there.

What it stores and where

It stores the goals you create, the days you move forward on them, the photos you choose and your settings (reminder time, theme and other options). All of it lives in the app's private folder on your phone. We don't see it and can't see it.

If you give a goal a photo of your own, the app keeps a cropped copy in that same private folder. It doesn't upload the photo anywhere. To pick it, it uses the system's own photo picker (Android or iPhone), which gives us no access to the rest of your gallery.

Permissions

Just one: showing notifications, and only if you choose a reminder time. The reminder is scheduled by the phone itself; there is no server behind it. You can turn it off in Options or in your phone's settings at any time.

What it doesn't do

It uses no analytics or advertising tools. There is no tracking, no user identifiers, and we share nothing with third parties, because we have nothing to share.

Backups

In Options you can export your data to a file and keep it wherever you like (your email, your cloud, another phone). That file is yours and goes wherever you send it; we never receive it.

Purchases

If you buy the premium version, the payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store, under their own terms and privacy policy. We don't see your payment details.

How to delete your data

Delete a goal in the app, or uninstall it: all the data goes with it, including any photos you added. Nothing is left anywhere else.

Your rights

Since we don't process your data, there is nothing for us to correct or erase: you have it all on your phone. Even so, if you have any question, write to jcgualde@apps4all.app. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or to the authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes and starts handling data differently, this page will change first, and we'll say so in the app. The date above shows the latest version.
