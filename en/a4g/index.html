One piece for every day you move forward.

Set a goal and pick a photo of what you want to achieve. The photo is cut into as many pieces as days you give yourself: 21, 30 or 66. When it's complete, the goal is yours.

Every piece is a day you moved forward. A bad day doesn't take any away: it only delays the photo.

How it works

  1. 1 Set a goal

    Give it a name and pick its photo. There are 31 to choose from, but your own works best: a picture of what you want to achieve.

  2. 2 Place today's piece

    Every day you move forward, you drag the piece into its gap. The gap lights up and the piece clicks into place.

  3. 3 You decide

    When the photo is complete, you choose: goal reached, off to your gallery of achievements. Or not yet, give me more days.

A bad day doesn't take pieces away.

No streaks to break, no punishments. A missed day only delays the puzzle. And if you moved forward but forgot to open the app, you can add the pieces for the past week.

Your goal never leaves your phone.

No account, no sign-up, no connection. No server, no analytics, no ads. To switch phones, you make a backup and take it with you.

Free to start.

The free version lets you have three goals at a time and one photo of your own. Premium removes the limits with a one-off payment, no subscription.
