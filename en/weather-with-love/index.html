Weather with love

A weather app with something more inside.

Weather with love gives you the real weather, with a widget and a line to start your day. And it keeps your Love notes: encrypted messages, photos and videos for the people you love.

  • Coming soon toGoogle Play
  • Coming soon toApp Store

For Android and iPhone.

The weather comes first.

It works with no account, no sign-up and without asking for your location. Search for your city and you're done.

  • Temperature, feels-like, humidity, wind and pressure.
  • A 3 or 7-day forecast, whichever you prefer.
  • Celsius or Fahrenheit, and kilometres or miles per hour.
  • A home screen widget that keeps itself up to date.
  • A line for every day. Don't like it? Tap it for another.

Your people, even when they're far away.

Add the people you love with their city. You'll see the weather there, with a line that suits their day. If it's raining in Berlin, it might be a good day to call Hugo.

And your Love notes, just for you.

When you create your user, you choose a secret city. Search for it in the weather and your notes open. Everyone picks their own, and we don't store it anywhere.

Your secret city
  • End-to-end encrypted. Only the recipient's phone can open them. Not even us.
  • Deleted from the server on arrival. Your conversations stay on the phones, never in the photo gallery.
  • No phone number or email. You connect with someone using an invitation code.
  • A signal only you understand. When someone writes to you, the widget changes a detail only you know. No notifications.
  • Photos, videos and groups. Up to 20 people per group.

Love cards worth collecting.

To send notes you use a love card for 1, 3 or 12 months. You pay once and it never renews by itself. To start, a 7-day one for free. There are 20 designs, each with a pair of little animals in a different kind of weather, and you can give them to your contacts.

Swipe to see all 20. We drew the illustrations ourselves.

For over-18s, with clear rules.

You can block anyone who bothers you and report a message right from the conversation. Whoever breaks the rules loses the ability to send. And when it comes to child abuse, zero tolerance.
