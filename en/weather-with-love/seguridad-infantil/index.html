Child safety standards

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Weather with love does not tolerate any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation. These are our standards and what we do to enforce them.

Adults only

Love notes are only for people aged 18 and over. Anyone creating a user confirms this when accepting the terms, and the app is rated for adults in the stores.

What is prohibited

  • Creating, sending, storing or requesting child sexual abuse material, whether real, drawn or computer-generated.
  • Contacting minors for sexual purposes (grooming), or trying to.
  • Sexualising minors in any way, sextortion, or helping others do any of these things.

How to report

In the app: long-press the message and choose Report. Or in your contacts, long-press the person and choose Report. They are blocked at once and their latest messages reach us for review. Outside the app: write to jcgualde@apps4all.app.

What we do

We review every report. If there are signs of child sexual abuse, we ban the account (neither it nor the other accounts on its phone can send again), we preserve the evidence without copying or sharing it, and we report it immediately to the Spanish National Police or Guardia Civil, as Spanish law requires. We cooperate with courts and authorities in everything they lawfully request.

Why we don't read messages

Messages are end-to-end encrypted: we cannot read them or review them in advance. That's why reports matter so much. If you receive something like this, report it from the app and don't forward it to anyone.

If a child is in danger

Call 112, the emergency number across the European Union, or your local emergency number.

Child safety contact

APPS 4 ALL, S. L., responsible for Weather with love: jcgualde@apps4all.app. We answer any question about these standards, whether from a user or an authority.
